Air charter broker disclosures

Chartavia is an air charter broker, not a direct air carrier. We don't own or operate aircraft, and we never have operational control of any flight. Every flight arranged through Chartavia is provided by a properly licensed direct air carrier: an operator that holds an FAA air carrier certificate for on-demand operations under Part 135 and keeps full operational control of its flights.

  • The capacity we act in. We act as an independent broker. We introduce travelers to operators and provide the tools to request, compare and accept offers. We don't buy or resell air transportation, we don't sign charter agreements for anyone, and we aren't a party to the charter agreement between a traveler and an operator.
  • Who operates your flight. When you accept an offer, we show you the name of the operator and its aircraft before you sign that operator's charter agreement.
  • Insurance. Chartavia does not hold liability insurance covering flights arranged through Chartavia. Operators carry the insurance that federal law requires of air carriers.
  • On request. Before you book, we'll tell you on request about any business relationship between Chartavia and an operator quoting your trip, the total you pay to or through Chartavia, and the third-party fees the operator will charge you directly.

1.What Chartavia does

Chartavia is a marketplace for private charter flights. Travelers post trip requests. Charter operators review those requests and send sealed offers, which other operators can't see. A traveler compares the offers, accepts one, and books the flight directly with that operator. Operators can also list empty legs, which are repositioning flights offered at a discount.

We provide the software, run the operator checks described in section 6, and help when something goes wrong. The operator you book with is responsible for your flight, including the aircraft, crew, safety, schedule, price and compliance with aviation law.

2.Your account

  • Keep your password secret. You're responsible for what happens under your account, so tell us right away if you think someone else has used it.
  • An operator's first account administers that operator's team. Administrators can invite team members with invite codes, and anything a team member does in Chartavia is done on behalf of that operator.

3.Requesting a charter

3.1 Your request

Describe your trip accurately, including the route, dates, number of passengers, baggage, pets, medical equipment, accessibility needs and anything else an operator needs to plan a safe flight. Operators price and plan from what you tell them. If details turn out to be wrong, an operator may change or withdraw its offer.

3.2 What operators see

Operators who can quote your trip see the request details you enter. Until you accept an offer, they see you only by your first name and last initial, and never your email address.

3.3 Deposits

  • Your first request is free. After that, each request needs a refundable deposit unless you're a Chartavia Plus member. We show the amount before you post. It depends on the largest aircraft category in your request and currently ranges from $150 to $500.
  • We refund your deposit in full if you close the request without accepting an offer, if no operator makes an offer within 72 hours, in which case the request closes automatically, or if, after you accept, the operator cancels the trip or the flight can't be operated for reasons that aren't your fault.
  • When you accept an offer, your deposit becomes Chartavia's fee for arranging the trip. It isn't refunded if you later cancel the trip yourself, unless the law requires a refund.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method. We make them promptly and within the time limits the law sets.

3.4 Offers

Each offer comes from an operator and shows the aircraft, the price and how long the offer stays open. Operators set their own prices. Chartavia doesn't set, guarantee or mark up any price. An offer may not include taxes and third-party charges, such as the federal excise tax, segment fees, airport and handling fees, de-icing or crew overnight costs. The operator's charter agreement says what's included.

3.5 Accepting an offer

Accepting an offer tells the operator you want to book it. It shows you the operator's name and aircraft, shows the operator your name, and lets you talk directly in Chartavia's chat. Your flight is booked only when you've signed the operator's charter agreement and the operator has confirmed the trip in Chartavia.

3.6 Paying for your flight

You pay the operator directly under its charter agreement. Chartavia doesn't collect, hold or add anything to the charter price. The operator's agreement governs payment, cancellation charges, changes, delays, baggage and the other terms of the flight.

3.7 Changes and cancellations

Arrange changes and cancellations with the operator under its charter agreement, and mark the trip cancelled in Chartavia. The operator's terms decide any charges on the charter price. Our deposit rules are in section 3.3.

3.8 Empty legs

Empty legs follow the operator's schedule, so they can change or disappear at short notice, even after you ask for one. Requesting an empty leg follows the same steps and rules as any other request.

3.9 Chartavia Plus

Chartavia Plus costs $79 a month and waives the deposit on every request. It renews monthly until you cancel. If you cancel, it stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't refund partial months unless the law requires it.

4.Quoting as an operator

4.1 Who can quote

To send offers or list empty legs, you must:

  • hold a valid FAA air carrier certificate authorizing on-demand operations under Part 135;
  • hold the economic authority the U.S. Department of Transportation requires for the flights you offer;
  • be a citizen of the United States as defined in 49 U.S.C. 40102(a)(15); and
  • pass the checks described in section 6.

4.2 What you may offer

Offer only flights you are authorized to operate yourself, on aircraft listed in your operations specifications (D085), flown by crews under your control. You may not offer another company's aircraft or act as a broker on Chartavia.

4.3 Your offers

Make every offer accurate and complete: the aircraft you will actually use, the price, what it includes and excludes, and any conditions. An offer is a good-faith commitment for as long as it stays open. If a traveler accepts, send them your charter agreement, confirm the trip in Chartavia once it's booked, and mark it complete after the flight.

4.4 Operational control and safety

You keep full operational control of every flight and make every safety decision. Nothing in Chartavia requires you to operate a flight you consider unsafe or unlawful. If you must cancel, tell the traveler and cancel the trip in Chartavia promptly. When an operator cancels an accepted trip, we refund the traveler's deposit.

4.5 Anonymity until acceptance

Until a traveler accepts your offer, don't put your company name, contact details, website or tail numbers in offers, notes or messages, and don't try to arrange the trip outside Chartavia. We hide contact details we detect, but you're responsible for what you send.

4.6 Keeping your documents current

Keep your certificate, D085 listing, aircraft and safety audit documents current in Chartavia. Tell us promptly if your certificate is suspended or revoked, if an aircraft leaves your operations specifications, or if a safety audit lapses or changes.

4.7 Your agreement with the traveler

Your charter agreement is between you and the traveler. You're responsible for its terms, for collecting payment, for taxes such as the federal excise tax, and for any refunds you owe under it or under the law.

4.8 Empty legs

List only real repositioning flights you intend to operate, and remove a listing as soon as it's no longer available.

4.9 Fees

Joining, quoting and listing on Chartavia are free, and we take no commission on charter payments. Operator Pro costs $299 a month and adds a 15-minute head start on new requests, priority placement in quote lists, unlimited team seats and 20 empty-leg listings. It renews monthly until you cancel and stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

4.10 Photos and documents

You confirm that you have the rights to the photos and documents you upload. You allow us to show your company profile, aircraft details, photos and verified safety rating to travelers as these Terms describe, and to review your documents as part of our checks.

6.How we check operators

Before an operator can send an offer or list an empty leg:

  • Certificate. We match its air carrier certificate number against the FAA's published list of Part 135 certificate holders.
  • Aircraft. We check each tail number against the FAA aircraft registry and against the aircraft the FAA lists on that certificate. An aircraft that isn't listed can be offered only after our staff clear it against the operator's D085.
  • Staff review. A member of our team reviews the account and its documents.

A safety rating from ARGUS, Wyvern or IS-BAO appears on an operator's offers only after our staff confirm it against the audit certificate, and only until that certificate's expiry date. Reviews come only from travelers whose trips the operator marked complete. Response times are measured from actual timestamps.

What our checks can't do. They rely on public records and documents at a point in time. We don't inspect aircraft, audit operations, check pilots or supervise flights, and a verified badge doesn't guarantee safety. Use your own judgment about any operator. We may decline, suspend or remove an operator at any time if a check fails, a document lapses or we believe travelers could be at risk.

7.Reviews

After an operator marks a trip complete, the traveler can review that operator once for that trip. Reviews must be honest and based on your own experience. Other users see the operator's average rating and number of reviews. We don't edit reviews, but we may remove ones that contain personal information, abuse or content unrelated to the trip, or that we believe are fake. Operators may not offer anything in return for a review or for changing one.
